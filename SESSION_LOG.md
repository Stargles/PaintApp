BlendMode is seeded with one case, the call CompositorOp already makes, so the buffer rule could be written whole. toggleFolderVisibility stopped writing through to descendants: the flag now gates the subtree, which is one behaviour in three places — the compositor walks the tree, isLayerEffectivelyVisible answers for the live canvas, and load migrates old projects. §10 item 3 was settled by the product owner as migrate, not load-as-is: an old hidden group is self-consistent on load and then comes back *empty* the first time the artist un-hides it, with nothing on screen explaining why. Session 26 — 2026-08-12: LAYER_COMPOSITING.md phase 5a — Tier 1 blend modes, less the sandwich. BlendMode moved out of LayerFolder.swift into its own file (four-point pbxproj registration, since PaintSoftwareUITests hand-lists its sources) and grew to fourteen separable cases; Layer.blendMode and its manifest field are defaulted, so existing projects and every Layer(...) call site are unchanged. "Clip to below" is deliberately not among them: §7 lists it in Tier 1 while saying it is not a blend but the mask machinery with an implicit source, so building it here would mean building phase 6's mask resolution early to serve one mode and rebuilding it when §6.2's AlphaMask arrives — deferred to phase 6 and flagged, so Tier 1 ships as fourteen modes rather than fifteen. **The headline finding is that CGBlendMode is not the authority.** Sweeping all fourteen over 4096 (colour, alpha) pairs put every mode within one step of the shader except colorDodge, colorBurn and softLight at 141, 249 and 16 — not a rounding regime but a different formula, CoreGraphics implementing the PDF 1.4 originals where the two divisions have no zero-backdrop guard (Color Dodge lifts a black backdrop to white where the modern rule keeps it black) and soft light uses a different D(cb) curve. W3C Compositing Level 1 added the guards and is what Photoshop and CSP do, so those three are hand-rolled on the CPU beside add/subtract/linearLight and the shader is the correct one; §5.1's "byte-for-byte definition of correct" is now scoped to the walk — order, buffers, alpha — and not the blend functions. After the fix, leaf and group sweeps read identical and every mode is within 1, softLight reaching 0 despite its sqrt being the sharpest evidence the diagnosis was right. The Metal backend stopped declining groups that need their own buffer, which phase 4 could survive only while a faded group was the sole trigger. PixelOps.rasterize is memoized on model state — §5.2 names it as the fix for the measured 276 ms snapshot and the sandwich structurally needs it — keyed on cel id, object identity *and* version for both tiers, because a cel id outlives the buffers under it: reopening a project rebuilds every RasterLayerTexture with its counter back at 0 under the same id, so a version-only key would serve pre-edit pixels. **Phase 5 is half done: the compositor blends and the thumbnail proves it, but reconcileLayers still hands Core Animation flat siblings, so a Multiply layer looks normal on the live canvas.** That is §5.2's sandwich and it is all that stands between this feature and being usable. Two worker sessions in isolated worktrees, both needing a fast-forward off origin/main first; the compositor worker hit a usage limit mid-task and its work was recovered uncommitted — its recorded delta table had been written before it could run the sweep and read 2 where the truth was 141, which is why the numbers here are measured. Fast tier 694/694; the full XCUITest suite was not re-run after the blend work.
