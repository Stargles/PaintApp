Session 56 — 2026-08-21: Closed `tmp/vec-undo` — **a vector layer's move/scale/rotate is undoable**, the BUGS.md entry three sessions had noticed and none had taken. `setVectorTransform` wrote the cel's `VectorCanvas.transform` and registered nothing, so Undo after a Move reached straight past it to whatever the artist did before. **The obvious fix is the wrong one and that is why it survived**: its call site `CanvasView.Coordinator.objectTransformChanged` fires on every touch-move, so a `recordUndo` there is hundreds of steps for one drag. **The bracket hangs off `isVectorTransforming`'s own `didSet`, on `CanvasManager`, not on the coordinator** — that is the whole design decision. Two paths clear that flag with *no* gesture ending (`rasterizeLayer`, and `handleActiveContextChanged` on a layer/frame switch); a coordinator-owned bracket needs both of them to remember to close it, and a bracket that leaks is worse than none, since it either drops the step or bills the artist's **next** drag to this one. Hung off the property observer, the flag cannot go false without the bracket closing, because going false *is* the close — `CanvasPresentationModifier`'s `.onDisappear`/`.onChange` interlock with the observer playing the modifier's part. **It opens on the gesture's first write, not when the flag turns on**, which buys three cases for free: a tap that moves nothing never opens one; an in-between cel is excluded without a second guard, because `objectTransformChanged` already refuses to write on one; and undo pressed mid-drag re-baselines, since `finalizePendingGesturesForHistoryAction` closes the bracket (the fill's and the shape's rule, which the transform needed too — there is no gesture end to hang a commit on, the artist stays in Move mode across an undo). **The captured cel is held by reference plus IDs, never by index**: both leak paths close *after* `currentLayerIndex` has moved or *after* the cel has dropped its `vector`, so a re-resolved index would name the wrong cel or none. **Ordering in `rasterizeLayer` is now load-bearing rather than tidy** — the clear stays above `withStructureUndo`, so the transform step is pushed before the rasterize step and Undo, popping last-first, walks back through the rasterize and only then the transform; the other order un-transforms a cel whose `vector` has not been put back. What the step stores is the `CGAffineTransform` on each side, and that is lossless because `setTransform` **replaces** rather than composes; redo is its mirror. **One cel, matching `setVectorTransform`** — a multi-cel vector layer transformed on two frames has genuinely had two `VectorCanvas` objects changed and wants two steps.
