Session 56 — 2026-08-21: Closed `tmp/vec-undo` — **a vector layer's move/scale/rotate is undoable**, the BUGS.md entry three sessions had noticed and none had taken. `setVectorTransform` wrote the cel's `VectorCanvas.transform` and registered nothing, so Undo after a Move reached straight past it to whatever the artist did before. **The obvious fix is the wrong one and that is why it survived**: its call site `CanvasView.Coordinator.objectTransformChanged` fires on every touch-move, so a `recordUndo` there is hundreds of steps for one drag. **The bracket hangs off `isVectorTransforming`'s own `didSet`, on `CanvasManager`, not on the coordinator** — that is the whole design decision. Two paths clear that flag with *no* gesture ending (`rasterizeLayer`, and `handleActiveContextChanged` on a layer/frame switch); a coordinator-owned bracket needs both of them to remember to close it, and a bracket that leaks is worse than none, since it either drops the step or bills the artist's **next** drag to this one. Hung off the property observer, the flag cannot go false without the bracket closing, because going false *is* the close — `CanvasPresentationModifier`'s `.onDisappear`/`.onChange` interlock with the observer playing the modifier's part. **It opens on the gesture's first write, not when the flag turns on**, which buys three cases for free: a tap that moves nothing never opens one; an in-between cel is excluded without a second guard, because `objectTransformChanged` already refuses to write on one; and undo pressed mid-drag re-baselines, since `finalizePendingGesturesForHistoryAction` closes the bracket (the fill's and the shape's rule, which the transform needed too — there is no gesture end to hang a commit on, the artist stays in Move mode across an undo). **The captured cel is held by reference plus IDs, never by index**: both leak paths close *after* `currentLayerIndex` has moved or *after* the cel has dropped its `vector`, so a re-resolved index would name the wrong cel or none. **Ordering in `rasterizeLayer` is now load-bearing rather than tidy** — the clear stays above `withStructureUndo`, so the transform step is pushed before the rasterize step and Undo, popping last-first, walks back through the rasterize and only then the transform; the other order un-transforms a cel whose `vector` has not been put back. What the step stores is the `CGAffineTransform` on each side, and that is lossless because `setTransform` **replaces** rather than composes; redo is its mirror. **One cel, matching `setVectorTransform`** — a multi-cel vector layer transformed on two frames has genuinely had two `VectorCanvas` objects changed and wants two steps. The no-op guard compares with a tolerance rather than `==`, because the overlay round-trips through `layerTransform(pivot:)`/`affine(from:pivot:)` (atan2 out, sin/cos back), so even a bare tap on a handle hands back a value that is equal to the eye and need not be bit-equal — an `==` there bills a tap as a move. `canUndo` joins `fillGestureActive`/`shapeGestureActive` and asks whether the layer has actually *moved*, not merely whether a bracket is open. **Tests: 13 new**, `VectorTransformUndoLogicTests`, all in the fast tier; the two leak-path tests each carry a **follow-up gesture**, because "the step arrived" does not prove the baseline was cleared and only the second drag landing on its own start does. Session 57 — 2026-08-21: Closed `tmp/text-stage4` — **Add Text stage 4**, ADD_TEXT.md §3's *"rotate, scale, and handles that are the right size"*. `Views/TextTransformOverlayView.swift` is nine grips — four corners, four edge midpoints, a rotation knob — in a **sibling** view pinned to `CanvasView`'s `container` and sitting above the editor, every dimension `screenPoints / canvasScale` pushed from the coordinator on each transform change (14 pt drawn, 44 pt hittable, 1 pt outline, 36 pt stand-off), nearest-within-reach hit-testing, raw `touchesBegan/Moved/Ended`, and the whole starting quad plus the anchor latched at touch-down. It holds layers and touches and **no geometry**: `handleLayout`, the nearest search, `anchor(for:)` and `TextFrameDrag` all live on `TextFrame`, which is `ShapeGeometry`'s own arrangement and the only reason any of it is testable. **Five things worth knowing.** *(1)* **The sketch's "one `recordUndo` per drag" is not what shipped**, and the departure is the session's undo model rather than a shortcut: a text session already registers exactly one step for everything inside it — `commitTextToVector`'s doc lists *dragging the box* among them — so a per-drag step lands **underneath** it, and the path is short: undo mid-session runs `finalizePendingGesturesForHistoryAction`, which commits the session and pushes its step, `history.undo()` reverts that, and the drag's step is then a **dead entry** whose next press restores a frame into a session that no longer exists. §2's *"an undo step that removes nothing is a step the artist has to press through"* is the same objection one level down. What the sketch was guarding against — `setVectorTransform`'s step-per-**delta** shape — is guarded and pinned: sixty deltas and one delta leave the same history depth, and the enclosing session registers exactly one step either way. *(2)* **Nine grips, not four**, because "independent-axis scale" is not expressible with corners alone and *set the wrap width, leave the height* is the common ask for type; the cost is four overlapping targets on a small box, which is what makes nearest-within-reach load-bearing rather than decorative. *(3)* **The rotation knob does not clear `autoSize` and the eight sizing grips do** — turning a box is not sizing it, and freezing a pristine box's growth because it was tilted would clip the next keystroke for a reason nobody could name; `TextFrame.resized(to:)` had to become rotation-preserving in the same session, since it rebuilt through `uprightCorners` and snapped a turned box straight on every keystroke. *(4)* **The glyphs actually turn**, which meant all three drawing paths (raster bake, vector flatten, live overlay) now concatenate `TextFrame.affineTransform` — the affine half of §1's homography — so a turned box draws with no bitmap and no resampling on the two CoreGraphics paths, and **byte-identically to before for an upright frame**, because there that matrix *is* the translate stages 1-3 wrote by hand. The caret rides the same matrix via `bounds`/`center`/`transform`, never `frame`, which is undefined on a transformed view. *(5)* **Stage 1's canvas-edge growth cap is gone**, as its own note promised, and removing it needed a second bound put back: `TextLayout.renderBox` floored its backing scale at 1, invisible only while the box was bounded in points, so a long title would have minted a bitmap as many pixels across as it is canvas points — the floor is now 0.05 and the overlay's cap is in **texels**, which is what §4 rule 1 actually says. **Tests: 27 new** — 26 in `TextTransformLogicTests` plus one in `TextLayoutLogicTests`, where the cap's test was rewritten into its opposite. Identities throughout, never measured glyph widths: the opposite corner does not move on a box turned 30°, a corner drag puts that corner under the finger, an edge grip freezes the axis it is not on, an overshoot **clamps rather than mirroring**, a rotation pivots without travelling and is invertible, and a quarter turn exchanges the ink's two axes in the baked pixels. The latch's own test is the sharp one: a keystroke landing **mid-drag** regrows a pristine box, so a drag re-reading the live frame each delta would come out with the regrown extents — and the measurement catches up on lift, so nothing typed is lost.
